## SECTION 1: B2B TRAVEL INDUSTRY FUNDAMENTALS

### Key Industry Terminology
- **GDS (Global Distribution System)**: Computer reservation systems used by travel agents to search and book travel inventory. Major GDS platforms include Amadeus, Sabre, and Travelport. KNOWAA Global provides direct API integration that complements GDS access.
- **OTA (Online Travel Agency)**: Online platforms like Booking.com, Expedia that sell travel to consumers. As a B2B partner, you bypass OTA markups and access net rates directly through KNOWAA Global.
- **Net Rate**: The wholesale price of a hotel room before markup. Travel agents add their margin on top. KNOWAA Global provides competitive net rates through direct hotel contracts.
- **Rack Rate**: The standard published rate for a hotel room without any discounts. This is the highest rate a hotel charges.
- **BAR (Best Available Rate)**: The lowest unrestricted rate available to the general public at any given time.
- **Allotment**: A block of rooms reserved for a travel agency or tour operator at a negotiated rate. KNOWAA Global maintains guaranteed allotments at 762+ partner properties.
- **FIT (Free Independent Traveler/Fully Independent Travel)**: Individual travelers not part of a group tour, booking customized itineraries. Most KNOWAA Global bookings are FIT.
- **GIT (Group Inclusive Tour)**: Group travel packages typically for 10+ travelers with negotiated group rates.
- **Release Date/Cut-off Date**: The deadline by which unsold allotment rooms are released back to the hotel for general sale.
- **Markup**: The percentage or fixed amount a travel agent adds to the net rate to determine the selling price to clients.
- **Commission**: The percentage of the booking value paid to the travel agent by the supplier. KNOWAA Global offers competitive commission structures.
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Rate adjustment based on demand, season, events, and market conditions. KNOWAA Global uses advanced algorithms for real-time competitive pricing.
- **Rate Parity**: The practice of maintaining consistent rates across distribution channels. KNOWAA Global ensures competitive rate parity through direct contracts.

### Distribution Chain in B2B Travel
The B2B travel distribution chain typically flows: Hotel → Wholesaler/Consolidator (KNOWAA Global) → Travel Agent → End Client. By working with KNOWAA Global, travel agents skip intermediaries and access rates closer to the source, maximizing their margins.

### Types of Travel Agency Models
- **Leisure Agencies**: Focus on vacation and holiday travel
- **Corporate Agencies**: Specialize in business travel management
- **MICE Agencies**: Handle Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions
- **Luxury/Specialty Agencies**: High-end, bespoke travel experiences (KNOWAA Global's primary partner profile)
- **Online Agencies**: Digital-first agencies operating primarily through web platforms

### Step 2: Rate Selection & Comparison
Understanding rate types available through KNOWAA Global:
- **Flexible Rate**: Higher price but allows free cancellation up to the deadline
- **Non-Refundable Rate**: Lower price but no cancellation refund
- **Early Bird Rate**: Discounted rate for bookings made well in advance (typically 30-90 days)
- **Last-Minute Rate**: Special pricing for near-date availability
- **Long Stay Rate**: Discounted rates for extended stays (typically 7+ nights)
- **Package Rate**: Bundled pricing including room + experiences/transfers
- Always review the cancellation policy before confirming the rate with your client
- Compare the net rate against your selling price to ensure adequate margin

## SECTION 3: HANDLING COMMON PROBLEMS & CHALLENGES

### Overbooking Situations
What to do when a hotel is overbooked and your client is affected:
1. Contact KNOWAA Global immediately at +1 786-452-4140
2. We will intervene directly with the hotel property
3. Standard hotel obligations during overbooking:
   - Provide alternative accommodation of equal or higher standard
   - Cover transportation to the alternative property
   - Provide complimentary meals and communication access
4. KNOWAA Global will follow up to ensure proper resolution
5. Document everything for potential compensation claims
6. We maintain escalation relationships with hotel management to prioritize our partner's guests

### Rate Discrepancies
If the charged rate differs from the booked rate:
1. Compare the booking confirmation against the invoice
2. Email support@knowaaglobal.com with both documents attached
3. Include the booking reference number and specific discrepancy details
4. We investigate directly with the hotel's revenue management team
5. Resolution typically within 2-3 business days
6. Adjustments are applied to your next settlement statement

### No-Show Management
When a guest doesn't arrive at the hotel:
- Hotels typically charge one night's room rate for no-shows
- To avoid no-show charges: cancel the booking before the cancellation deadline
- If the guest was delayed (flight issues, emergencies), contact us immediately — we can often negotiate with the hotel
- For no-shows due to documented emergencies, we work with hotels to waive or reduce charges
- Always confirm your client's travel plans 24-48 hours before check-in

### Guest Complaints During Stay
Escalation process for guest complaints:
- **Level 1 — Direct Resolution**: Guest contacts hotel front desk. Most issues (towels, minibar, AC) resolved within 30 minutes.
- **Level 2 — KNOWAA Support**: If hotel doesn't resolve, contact support@knowaaglobal.com or call +1 786-452-4140. We contact hotel management directly.
- **Level 3 — Account Manager Escalation**: For persistent issues or VIP guests, your dedicated account manager intervenes with senior hotel leadership.
- **Level 4 — Emergency Relocation**: In extreme cases (health/safety, unacceptable conditions), we arrange immediate alternative accommodation.
- Document all complaints with dates, times, names, and photos if applicable.
- Post-stay compensation (room credit, discount on future booking) can be negotiated through KNOWAA Global.

### Late Check-In / Early Check-Out
- Late check-in: Inform the hotel via KNOWAA Global support to hold the reservation
- Early check-out: Depending on rate type, may result in early departure charges
- Guaranteed late check-in can be requested at booking time
- Early check-out policies vary by hotel — always verify before promising to clients

## SECTION 5: MIAMI & SOUTH FLORIDA — DESTINATION EXPERTISE

### Miami Seasonal Guide
- **High Season (December-April)**: Peak demand, highest rates, advance booking essential. Major events: Art Basel (December), Miami Music Week (March), Ultra Music Festival (March). Average temperature: 75-82°F (24-28°C).
- **Shoulder Season (May-June, November)**: Moderate demand, good value. Pleasant weather, fewer crowds.
- **Low Season (July-October)**: Hurricane season, lowest rates, some hotels offer significant discounts. Higher humidity, afternoon thunderstorms common. Hurricane preparedness is important.
- **Year-Round Events**: Formula 1 Miami Grand Prix (May), Miami Open Tennis (March), Miami Boat Show (February), Art Deco Weekend (January).

### Miami Hotel Districts Guide
- **South Beach (SoBe)**: Art Deco boutique hotels and large beachfront resorts. Walking distance to Ocean Drive, Lincoln Road. Best for: couples, nightlife, beach lovers. Price range: $200-$1,500/night.
- **Mid-Beach**: Upscale resorts, quieter than South Beach. Faena District, luxury properties. Best for: families, luxury seekers. Price range: $300-$2,000/night.
- **Bal Harbour**: Ultra-luxury, exclusive shopping, pristine beach. Best for: high-end clients, shopping enthusiasts. Price range: $400-$3,000/night.
- **Downtown/Brickell**: Business district, modern high-rises, vibrant dining scene. Best for: business travelers, urban explorers. Price range: $150-$600/night.
- **Coconut Grove**: Relaxed waterfront setting, boutique properties. Best for: families, nature lovers. Price range: $200-$500/night.
- **Coral Gables**: Historic elegance, The Biltmore, upscale residential feel. Best for: golfers, culture enthusiasts. Price range: $200-$800/night.
- **Key Biscayne**: Island resort setting, secluded beaches. Best for: beach families, tennis enthusiasts. Price range: $300-$1,000/night.
- **Wynwood/Design District**: Trendy, art-focused, emerging hotel market. Best for: millennials, art lovers, foodies. Price range: $150-$400/night.
- **Fort Lauderdale**: Beachfront resorts, cruise port proximity, yachting capital. Best for: cruise pre/post stays, boating enthusiasts. Price range: $150-$800/night.

### Miami Transportation Guide
- **Miami International Airport (MIA)**: Main international gateway, 20-40 min to Miami Beach depending on traffic.
- **Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International (FLL)**: Alternative airport, often cheaper flights, 45-60 min to Miami Beach.
- **Ground Transportation**: Uber/Lyft widely available, private transfers recommended for VIP clients, water taxis in Fort Lauderdale.
- **Parking**: Hotel valet typically $40-$75/day in Miami Beach. Street parking limited. Rental car recommended only if exploring beyond Miami Beach.

### Miami Dining & Experiences
- **Michelin-Recognized**: Miami's dining scene has grown significantly, with numerous acclaimed restaurants.
- **Celebrity Chef Restaurants**: Multiple locations in South Beach, Brickell, and Design District.
- **Cuban Cuisine**: Authentic experiences in Little Havana, Calle Ocho.
- **Seafood**: Joe's Stone Crab (seasonal October-May), Garcia's Seafood, various waterfront options.
- **Nightlife**: LIV at Fontainebleau, E11EVEN, Basement at the Edition — table reservations essential.
- KNOWAA Global can arrange exclusive dining reservations and VIP nightlife access through our Premium Experience Network.

## SECTION 6: BAHAMAS — DESTINATION EXPERTISE

### Bahamas Overview
- **Location**: Atlantic Ocean archipelago, 50 miles east of Florida, 700+ islands
- **Main Tourist Islands**: New Providence (Nassau/Paradise Island), Grand Bahama (Freeport), Exumas, Eleuthera/Harbour Island, Abaco
- **Currency**: Bahamian Dollar (BSD), pegged 1:1 with USD. US dollars widely accepted.
- **Language**: English
- **Entry Requirements**: Valid passport required. US citizens no visa needed for stays up to 90 days.
- **Time Zone**: Eastern Standard Time (same as Miami)
- **Getting There from Miami**: Direct flights from MIA (45-60 min), ferry services available, private charters and yacht crossings

### Bahamas Seasonal Guide
- **High Season (December-April)**: Best weather, highest demand. Average temperature: 75-82°F. Book 60-90 days in advance.
- **Shoulder Season (May-June, November)**: Good weather, fewer crowds, better rates.
- **Hurricane Season (June-November)**: Lower rates, risk of tropical weather. Travel insurance strongly recommended.
- **Spring Break (March)**: Nassau/Paradise Island sees significant demand from college travelers.
- **Junkanoo Festival**: December 26 and January 1 — vibrant cultural celebration, hotels book quickly.

### Bahamas Property Types
- **Nassau/Paradise Island**: Large resorts (Atlantis, Baha Mar), casino properties, water parks. Best for families and groups.
- **Exumas**: Ultra-exclusive private island resorts, swimming pigs experience. Best for luxury seekers, honeymooners.
- **Harbour Island/Eleuthera**: Pink sand beaches, boutique luxury, intimate settings. Best for couples, romantic getaways.
- **Grand Bahama**: Value-oriented, eco-tourism, diving. Best for adventure travelers, budget-conscious clients.
- **Private Islands**: Exclusive resort islands with all-inclusive luxury. Ultimate privacy and exclusivity.
- KNOWAA Global has direct contracts with premier Bahamas properties and can arrange seamless Miami-Bahamas combinations.

### Bahamas Experiences
- Swimming with nurse sharks at Compass Cay
- The famous swimming pigs at Big Major Cay, Exumas
- Thunderball Grotto snorkeling (James Bond filming location)
- Dean's Blue Hole diving in Long Island
- Pink sand beaches of Harbour Island

## SECTION 15: GLOSSARY OF ADDITIONAL TRAVEL INDUSTRY TERMS

- **ADR (Average Daily Rate)**: Average revenue per paid occupied room
- **RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)**: Total room revenue divided by available rooms
- **Occupancy Rate**: Percentage of available rooms occupied during a period
- **Run of House (ROH)**: Room type assigned at check-in at hotel's discretion
- **Complimentary (Comp) Room**: Free room provided by hotel, often for group leaders
- **Day Use Rate**: Rate for room usage during daytime only (no overnight stay)
- **Connecting Rooms**: Adjacent rooms with a door between them (ideal for families)
- **Adjoining Rooms**: Rooms next to each other without a connecting door
- **Guaranteed Reservation**: Booking held all night with guest's credit card guarantee
- **Walk-In**: Guest arriving without a reservation
- **Turndown Service**: Evening service where bed is prepared for sleeping
- **Club Level/Executive Floor**: Premium hotel floor with exclusive lounge, amenities, and services
- **All-Inclusive**: Rate includes room, meals, drinks, and selected activities
- **MAP (Modified American Plan)**: Rate includes room, breakfast, and dinner
- **EP (European Plan)**: Room only, no meals included
- **CP (Continental Plan)**: Room with continental breakfast
- **AP (American Plan)**: Room with all meals included
- **SGL/DBL/TPL/QAD**: Single/Double/Triple/Quadruple occupancy
- **Pax**: Number of passengers/guests
- **Rooming List**: Document listing all guests in a group with room assignments
- **Black-Out Dates**: Dates when special rates or allotments are not available
- **Yield Management**: Revenue optimization through pricing adjustments based on demand
- **Channel Manager**: Software that manages hotel inventory across multiple distribution channels
- **PMS (Property Management System)**: Software used by hotels to manage operations
- **CRS (Central Reservation System)**: Centralized system for managing hotel reservations
