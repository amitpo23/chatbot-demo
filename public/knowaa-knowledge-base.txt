Knowaa Global is a dynamic, technology-first travel operator specializing in high-end hospitality distribution. ## PRODUCT PORTFOLIO & CORE DESTINATIONS

### Miami & South Florida (Core Market)
Extensive inventory in 4-5 star properties across Miami Beach, Downtown Miami, Brickell, Coral Gables, and Fort Lauderdale. Peak demand occurs during the winter season (December-April), where our direct contracts provide crucial availability access during sold-out periods.

**Key Neighborhoods & Areas:**
- **South Beach**: Iconic beaches, Art Deco architecture, vibrant nightlife
- **Wynwood**: Street art, galleries, trendy restaurants and bars
- **Brickell**: Urban sophistication, luxury shopping, fine dining
- **Coconut Grove**: Bohemian charm, waterfront dining, lush gardens
- **Coral Gables**: Mediterranean-style architecture, upscale shopping
- **Key Biscayne**: Secluded beaches, nature parks, resort living
- **Miami Beach**: Beachfront hotels, ocean-view dining, water sports
- **Design District**: Luxury fashion, contemporary art, upscale dining
- **Fort Lauderdale**: Beach resorts, cruise port proximity, boating culture

### New York City (Secondary Stronghold)
Year-round high-demand market with rapidly expanding footprint and direct relationships. Coverage includes Manhattan, Brooklyn, and major business districts.

### Bahamas
Seamless island getaways coordinated from Miami. Hidden beaches, exclusive island resorts, yacht charter island-hopping experiences combining the best of Miami and the Bahamas.

### Seasonal Considerations
- **Peak Winter Season (Miami/South Florida)**: December through April — advance booking recommended
- **Year-Round Demand (NYC)**: Consistent demand with spikes during major events and conferences
- **Forecast Planning**: Partners sharing group requirements or peak-date needs receive priority inventory allocation
